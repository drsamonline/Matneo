Questions

Before you buy

What is sold here?

Books written by Dr. Sohil Momin — clinical manuals, materia medica and poetry — a course for the NTET, and the three Forge software licences. The free apps live on the project hub.

Where do the books come from?

Amazon. Each book page links to its own listing, and the price shown is the list price in US dollars — the retailer can change it. Paperbacks and Kindle editions are separate entries because they are separate purchases.

How is the course delivered?

As one HTML file, sent by email after UPI payment. It runs offline in any browser and is licensed for 365 days from first activation.

Are there free resources?

Yes — Free resources collects the tools and downloads that cost nothing.

Support the work

Matneo is one person's work: no company, no ads, no tracking. If something here saved you an afternoon, a contribution keeps the next one coming — any amount, or none.
