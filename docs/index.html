FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is MATNEO?

MATNEO is an AI-powered digital products and automation platform. It creates practical digital planners, templates, AI resources and browser-based tools for better work and life.

What kinds of products does MATNEO offer?

The catalogue is organised into Templates, Books, AI Resources, HTML Apps and Printables, plus a Free Resources section.

Do the tools work offline?

HTML Apps are offline tools that work directly in your browser — no install and no build step required.

Are there free resources?

Yes. A Free Resources section collects tools and downloads created to help you get started.

How much do products cost?

Featured products currently range from about ₹199 to ₹499, and free resources are also available.

How do I browse the full catalogue?

Open the live demo or the repository on GitHub to explore every category and product.
