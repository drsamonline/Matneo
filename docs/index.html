FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does MATNEO sell?

MATNEO sells practical digital products: printable planners, business templates, AI prompt packs, books and offline browser-based tools.

How are products delivered?

Every product is digital and delivered instantly — there is nothing to install and no build step. HTML apps run directly in your browser.

What categories are available?

The shop is organised into HTML Apps, Homeopathy, Health Tools, Games, Utilities and Data, plus a Free Resources section.

Are there free resources?

Yes. A Free Resources section collects tools and downloads created to help you get started at no cost.

How much do products cost?

Featured products currently range from about ₹199 to ₹499, and free resources are also available.

How do I add new products?

The catalogue is driven by a single data file, data/products.json, so new products are added by editing that file.
